# Contextual saint relationships

`household` (Household) records a supported association with another saint's
household. It does not assert biological kinship. `encountered` (Encountered)
records a documented meeting or visit, without asserting discipleship.
Both are symmetric: either profile shows the same relationship label. Use a
sourced public note to explain who lived in whose household or the circumstances
of the encounter. Public notes must not include museum or relic information.

These contextual types do not automatically change museum family membership,
section, tier or placement. Explicit curatorial grouping remains separate.
