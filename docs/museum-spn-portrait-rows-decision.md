# SPN vitrine contents design decision

Paragata Dasi selected Portrait rows on 24 October 2026.

- Use one vertical list, never a multi-column grid of saints.
- Place the list to the right of the whole-room museum map when space permits.
- Keep the restored left navigation. Add no further panels beside the map and contents.
- On narrower screens, stack contents beneath the map rather than squeezing the map or names.
- Retain shelf grouping and quiet separators; show published portraits where available and a subdued empty image space otherwise.
- Preserve the one shared explanation of source-recorded locations instead of repeating “Source location, unverified” per saint.

The original bullet list was rejected as monotonous. Quiet cards were not selected; portrait rows provide distinction with less visual weight.

Implemented alongside the curator review flow. This decision supersedes the earlier full-width map with contents always beneath it in the queued navigation refinement. Prototype shelf assignments are illustrative and must not enter production data.
