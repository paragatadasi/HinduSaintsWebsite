# SPN tier and story consistency

Review started on 28 October 2026 after the curator found inherited tiers that did not match the current East–North vitrine stories.

## Findings

The initial pass covered the 82 proposed cases. The immutable proposal retained older tiers: some Primaries were inferred family heads, while independently prominent historical teachers remained Secondary or Tertiary. Family-head status alone does not establish prominence, and an unfamiliar name does not justify demotion. Preserve explicit archivalist and curator choices while checking evidence.

The live planner inventory already reads effective tiers. Two presentation paths still used captured tiers: the explorer's Primary anchor names and the starter guide's entry labels. Those can disagree with a subsequently reviewed SPN tier.

The fix projects current, unambiguously matched SPN tiers into the starter's presentation and derives the current proposal's anchor names from reconciled rows. It preserves guide IDs, digests, group membership, destinations and decisions. Historical proposal anchor metadata remains historical. Conflicting or ambiguous identities retain the captured guide tier with an explicit review note; they do not receive a guessed tier.
