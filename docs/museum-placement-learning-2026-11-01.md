# Museum placement and move-flow learning · 1 November 2026

This records reusable lessons from the SPN refinement. Dated proposal exports and the current companion remain the sources for rosters, current locations and accepted moves. This document does not allocate a saint or confirm a move.

## Separate the decisions

Keep five independent facts: source inventory evidence; canonical identity and historical relationships; reviewed display membership and proposed destination; confirmed physical location; and the version actually serving in the reviewer/companion. A source row is not necessarily a saint or a verified object. A saved proposal is not a completed move, and a successful public release dispatch is not proof that the new private serving image is live.

Use exact source and canonical IDs for joins. Preserve original locations when recording current locations. A later specific curator decision takes precedence over an inherited broad roster; compare exact membership and versions before applying a rotation. If a save result is uncertain, read it back before retrying. One agreed writer should own a batch, so concurrent collaborators do not replay each other's changes.

## Make geographic evidence answer the current question

For every display member, record a placement reason before drawing the map:

- **Locality-led:** an accepted city/site association directly drives this placement.
- **Family-led:** an accepted personal or institutional branch drives placement; an explicitly attributed display anchor may orient the map without becoming a claim that every member lived there.
- **Additional connection:** birthplace, other life/work center, visit, collection place or a related family's location. Preserve its role and provenance rather than promoting all linked places into placement drivers.
- **Unresolved:** the driver is not established. Keep the holding in the roster, but do not manufacture a point from its host case, surname or nearest known city.

The default arrangement map shows placement drivers. Additional connections are an optional layer. A click should reveal both the reason for the displayed point and the record's other associations. Curator recollections remain attributed and qualified until checked; they can support a provisional display rationale without overwriting canonical biography.

Do not simply suppress inconvenient distant points. An explicit outlying city cohort with no documented host-region reason is a real review case. Anandi Bai's tentative museum-label recollection supports the Vrindavan driver while Amritsar remains a recorded birthplace connection.

## Tell the route through anchors, then locality blocks

Read the actual roster before naming a case. Prioritize immediate family/teacher branches and securely identified same-person holdings. Put a recognizable lead story in each useful viewing position, then place coherent city cohorts around it. Do not make adjacent city saints into disciples, or make several branches one transitive guru chain.

Before crowding a case or creating a case of only unresolved infill, compare whole-group distributions across the available neighbors. Balance stories and coherent places, not merely equal entry counts. A small strong headline case can be intentional; leave its unused estimated load as potential flexible space rather than inventing infill or a measured capacity claim.

Use the evidence's useful locality scale. City-level grouping suits many regions; Vraj may benefit from Barsana, Govardhan/Radha Kund, Mathura/Gokul and Vrindavan subareas when they explain the visitor's story. Preserve finer source places even when cards are consolidated at the driver level. Consolidate moved cards within their receiving cases so duplicate locality cards do not disguise a single cohort.

## Regions, bridges and physical numbers

A display region is navigation and orientation, not a rewrite of a saint's tradition or every associated place. Current regional colors must follow the accepted physical-case roster, including cross-island assignments, rather than an old row label or phase owner.

A meaningful bridge may belong to both region filters while remaining one case and one set of holdings. Base those memberships on the lead story, not every incidental biography location. A single intermediate color is helpful for adjacent regional transitions; a distant cross-cutting connection need not pretend to be the next step in a one-dimensional rainbow. Always expose the region labels, since color alone is insufficient.

Keep proposed display numbers separate from stable physical-case IDs. Preview current/proposed labels side by side and recheck the crosswalk against later case swaps before migration. A spectrum inspired by a proposed walking route does not authorize renumbering, movement or a new canonical region.

Regional names are contextual: tourism, administrative councils and cultural zones use different boundaries. Compare the actual placement-driver footprint and name the museum's curatorial scope explicitly; do not claim one convention is the sole national definition.

## Make the move executable under real constraints

Use the latest curator shelf policy and preserve actual recorded placements. Check the permitted living-saint band separately from whole-case totals. Unknown dimensions are additional uncertainty, never zero footprint; inherited source-shelf shares are comparison estimates, not measured geometry.

Respect fixed objects and mixed housing at the item/component scope supported by evidence. Plan a case without a protected large object only when the user has authorized that scenario. Keep filling conditional until its relocation is actually confirmed outside the case; a missing or unknown location is not evidence of removal. The dependency should release after a valid recorded relocation, not remain a permanent static warning.

For staging efficiency, simulate the next steps against current locations: which incoming cases consume staging, which transfers can go directly to a prepared receiver, and which clearances produce a temporary peak. Count a full clear separately from an outgoing-only transfer, and do not claim that a superseded proposal makes a physical case empty.

## Verify individual coverage and the visible release

A colored case or a visible shelf tab is not proof that all of its current eligible holdings have recommendations. Audit every eligible exact source: give it a conceptual starting shelf, an explicit retain-current state, a context-only state, or a specific fit/identity hold. Context-only figures and unconfirmed holdings must not acquire physical confirmation actions.

Project the same approved roster, headline membership, region, source guards, trees and shelf plan through the reviewer, companion, room map and exports. Headline labels must survive only while their named anchor sources remain in that case. Preserve richer existing trees and distinguish teacher, family, friendship, affiliation and distant tradition connections in their line styles and labels.

Check authenticated live navigation, representative headlines/shelves and protected dependencies after deployment. Report saved native decisions, integrated code, dispatched deployment and verified live behavior separately. Do not call a new guide live because an earlier batch has appeared.
